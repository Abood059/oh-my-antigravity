# Skill Intensity Levels

Each skill you load carries an intensity level. The level tells you how much focus and priority to give that skill.

## high

This skill has the highest priority. Treat it as your primary task.
Give it your full attention and scrutiny. Do not skip any of its rules.
If this skill conflicts with a lower-intensity skill, this one wins.

## medium

This skill has normal priority. Follow it with standard focus.
If it conflicts with a `high` skill, the `high` skill wins.

## low

This skill has lower priority. Use it as a secondary reference.
If it conflicts with a `high` or `medium` skill, that one wins.

## Priority Order

high > medium > low

## How to Read Your Skills

Your prompt lists each skill as:

    - <path> (intensity: <level>)

Read the skill file at <path>.
Apply it at the level given.
